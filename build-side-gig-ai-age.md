# Building a Side Gig in the Age of AI – Business Roadmap

## Phase 1: Pick Your Niche

**Start here:** What micro-niche do you already belong to?

- Where do you have unfair distribution advantages?
- What community do people in this space already trust you in?
- What pain point do you see repeatedly that no one has solved?

**Red flags:**
- "I want to build something cool and then find the audience"
- "I don't know this space yet but it seems profitable"

**Green flags:**
- You're already part of this community
- You spend time there anyway
- You know the pain points intimately

## Phase 2: Map the Money (Most Important Phase)

**This is where you validate if people will actually pay.**

### Step 1: Talk to 10-20 People in Your Niche

- Find them where they already hang out (Discord, Reddit, Slack communities)
- Ask: "What's the biggest pain point you have with [X]?"
- Listen for specificity. Write down exact quotes.
- Ask: "How much time does this cost you per week/month?"
- Ask: "Would you pay for a tool that solved this? How much?"

**What you're looking for:** Do people spontaneously mention the same problem? Do they have budget/willingness to pay?

### Step 2: Identify Monetization Levers

Don't think about features yet. Think about what they actually value.

**Ask yourself:**
- Which part of their workflow causes the most friction?
- What would save them the most time?
- What would make them look better to their boss/team?
- What's the cost of *not* solving this? (in time, money, stress)

**Example problem:** Project managers spend 4 hours per week wrestling with stakeholder requirements, approval processes, and technical constraints. They can't automate this away with AI. They'd pay $50-200/month for a tool that made this 2 hours.

### Step 3: Build One Landing Page

Use Framer or manual HTML. Write:
- The exact pain point (use their words)
- Your solution in one sentence
- Why you're the one building this
- Price (pick a number)
- "Notify me when it's ready" email capture

**Post it in your niche communities.** Watch what happens. Did anyone sign up?

If yes → move forward
If no → talk to more people. Re-read their feedback.

## Phase 3: Build the Minimal MVP

**Constraint:** Nights and weekends only. Build only what proves you solve the problem.

### Tech Stack (Pick One)

- **Lovable.dev** (easiest) - No/low code, produces working websites, Stripe integration built-in
- **Lovable + Outa** (if you need backend) - Add authentication, payments, CRM
- **Vercel** (if you need custom code) - Git-based deployment

### What to Build

Focus on ONE core workflow that causes the most pain. Ignore everything else.

**Example:** If the pain is "wrestling with stakeholder requirements," build a tool that:
1. Captures requirements from multiple people (form + comments)
2. Organizes them by priority/category
3. Shows you when conflicts exist

That's it. Don't build: approval workflows, AI suggestions, integrations, reporting, export. Build it later if people want it.

### Success Metric

Can you do your core workflow in the tool 30% faster than doing it manually?

If yes → ship
If no → cut scope again

## Phase 4: Launch & Collect Money

### Launch Plan

1. **Email the waitlist** from your landing page. Offer early access or discount.
2. **Post in your communities** (the ones where you did customer research). "Hey, I built this for the problem we talked about. Here's how to use it."
3. **Collect payment immediately.** Use Stripe (via Lovable or Outa). Charge something, even if it's $29/month. This tests if people actually value it.

### Track These Numbers

- **Conversion rate:** Signups → paid customers
- **Churn rate:** Are people staying or leaving?
- **Time on each workflow:** Are people actually using it?
- **Feedback:** Where do they abandon? What's broken?

### Iterate by Listening

- Read every piece of feedback
- Watch videos of people using your product (ask 5 customers to screen-record)
- Ask: "What would make you use this 2x per week instead of 1x?"
- Ship changes weekly
- Measure conversion immediately after each change

This is where you learn which features are worth money and which are noise.

## Phase 5: Scale Your Business

Once you have 10-15 paying customers:

- Double down on the one workflow they love most
- Add features based on churn risk, not guesses
- Increase price (if churn is low, you underpriced)
- Focus your marketing on the exact niche (not everyone)

**Expected timeline:** 2-3 months to paying customers. 6-12 months to $500-1000/month MRR.
